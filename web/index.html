Quantitative Methodology & SEBI Benchmark Standards

1. Multi-Agency Composite Scoring (0–100)

Rather than relying on a single rating agency, funds are scored using a weighted synthesis across 5 major analytical frameworks:

Morningstar Stars: 20%
Morningstar Medalist: 10%
Value Research Stars: 20%
CRISIL CMFR Rank: 20%
1 Finance Score: 15%
Advisorkhoj Volatility: 15%

* Value Research ratings (1–5 Stars), Risk Grades, and 3-Year Average Ratings are parsed directly from the official Mutual Fund Insight magazine scorecard (Page 85 onwards) stored in the reference folder.

3. CAPM & Capture Ratio Formulas

  • Beta (β): Covariance of fund daily returns with category benchmark / Variance of benchmark.
  • Jensen's Alpha (α): Annualized excess return over CAPM expectations using India 10Y G-Sec yield (Rf = 7.0%):
    Alpha = CAGR_fund - [Rf + Beta * (CAGR_benchmark - Rf)]
  • Morningstar Monthly Capture Ratios: Compounded monthly returns during positive months (Upside Capture) and negative months (Downside Capture). An Overall Capture Ratio > 1.0 proves superior upside participation with downside defense.
